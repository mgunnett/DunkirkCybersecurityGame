# DunkirkCybersecurityGame

**Brief gameplay description:**
	You are a British citizen, living off the coast of the English Channel. One day, you receive the strangest call, from the British Ministry of Shipping, requesting to collect your motorboat. As an experience boatman, you agree, on the condition that you will get to sail your own vessel. The Ministry agrees--although hesitant--and explains that your boat will be used to sail across the Channel to the beaches of Dunkirk, where thousands of men on the Allied Powers are awaiting rescue from the overwhelming German forces. With your boating and navigation skills, you set sail from Ramsgate to Dunkirk, where the soldiers await your rescue.

**Gameplay mechanics:**
    - The journey from Ramsgate, UK, to Dunkirk Beach took about 10 hours. Making each minute into one second will result in a 10 minute game.
    - The player will be given messages detailing the route for them to travel, but some messages have been spoofed by German forces. They are trying to steer the player in the wrong direction by giving them false information, and it is up to the player to find the Allied messages and follow the correct course.
    - The player will have no knowledge of their destination, so they are reliant on the messages. They will steer the boat a certain degrees.
    - The player will have a rule book that guides them on distinguishing legitimate messages from fakes.
